YGwar - Albania (ALB) added.  Built by tools/albania_partition.py -> albania_map.py -> albania_files.py (+ build_republic_content.py, render_full_map.py).

WHAT WAS ADDED
- 35 land provinces (36 districts on the source map minus Malesi e Madhe, see below), 12 states (121..132 = the 12 counties), tag ALB.
- map/provinces.bmp, heightmap.bmp, world_normal.bmp edited in the Albania area only. definition.csv: 18 ids of sea provinces that Albania
  covered completely (972 973 974 1000 1001 1028 1029 1054 1055 1080 1081 1107 1108 1110 1137 1138 1169 1170) are REUSED for Albanian land
  provinces (so no id renumbering), 17 new ids 1246..1262 were appended. Ids are still sequential.
- map/strategicregions/3_albania.txt (new), the 18 reused ids removed from 2_adriatic.txt.
- positions.txt, unitstacks.txt (39 rows per province), buildings.txt (random positions per state), supply_nodes.txt (Tirane, Durres),
  railways.txt (4 lines), airports.txt (Tirane) extended.
- history/countries/ALB - Albania.txt (communism, 55%), history/units/ALB_OOB.txt (12 infantry divisions), leader ALB_YGwar_leader
  (Ramiz Alia, ideology = stalinism), AI block, unit names, MIO tag lists, colour, tag registration.
- ALB focus tree: 20 focuses, 4 national spirits (generated like the other 11 trees), en/ru localisation, state names and VP names.

COMPROMISES / THINGS TO CHECK IN-GAME
1. Malesi e Madhe (NW tip) is gone: at this scale it falls completely under the existing Montenegrin coast. Shkodër and Tropoje are small.
   To get it back the south tip of Montenegro must be cut.
2. Albania is drawn at 93% of its real size (scale 0.35), its east/north edges were stretched up to ~69 px to touch
   Montenegro/Kosovo/Macedonia and clipped by them (9% of the area).
3. buildings.txt: 6 rows (naval_base_spawn, floating_harbor, naval_base of states 13 and 70) pointed at sea provinces that no longer
   exist and were removed. Those two states lose a naval base position.
4. Coastal flag recomputed for every land province: 1 existing province changed.
5. Heightmap/normal map in Albania are generated (province terrain + noise), rivers/trees/cities layers are empty (as in the rest of the mod).
6. Building positions are random points inside each state; state files contain no buildings (like your other states).
7. No flag files added: ALB uses the vanilla ALB flags and names. Check that the vanilla ALB tag does not clash with the mod tag in your game.
8. Leader ideology 'stalinism' is assumed to exist in vanilla; if error.log complains, change it in common/characters/YGwar_leaders.txt.
9. Not tested in the game (no HOI4 here). Please send error.log after the first start.
